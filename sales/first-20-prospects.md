# KODYRA — First 20 rental-agency conversations

## Objective

Start **20 carefully selected, relevant conversations**; the target is replies and discovery calls, not unsolicited mass messaging. A "qualified lead" must have an identifiable business need and a contact willing to discuss it.

## Targeting

Start with local independent car-rental agencies in Morocco that have a public business contact and an identifiable booking process. Expand internationally only after we have a clear English-language proposal and can support that client's working hours, language, payments and delivery requirements.

Do not infer that a business has poor operations from its website or reviews. Discovery questions are for learning, not asserting problems.

## Research fields per prospect

Business name, city/country, website, publicly listed business contact, role if known, booking channels visible on their own site, one *verified* reason the FleetOps workflow might be relevant, date contacted, response, next step, and permission to follow up. Never copy private emails or scraped personal data into public GitHub.

## Lightweight sequence

1. Research and personalize for a relevant business before contact.
2. Send one courteous, specific introductory message using an appropriate business channel.
3. If no reply, at most one polite follow-up after a reasonable interval; respect opt-outs.
4. Track replies privately, not in this public repository.
5. Offer a short demonstration only if interest is expressed.
6. Agree on a written project scope before quoting delivery dates or commitments.

## Measure without vanity metrics

Count relevant contacts, replies, discovery calls, written proposals, accepted projects and reasons for declining. Review message relevance and product fit, rather than increasing outreach volume indiscriminately.
